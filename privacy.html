隱私權政策

最後更新日期：2026 年 8 月 10 日

本頁說明算紙（Suanzhi，網址 suanzhii.netlify.app，以下稱「本站」）如何處理使用者資料。使用本站即表示您同意本政策所述的做法。

一、本站不會蒐集的資料

本站所有計算工具都在使用者的瀏覽器中執行。您在計算器中輸入的任何數值，包括成績、學分、土壤參數與工程數據，都不會傳送到本站或任何第三方的伺服器，也不會被儲存或記錄。

本站不設置會員系統，不要求也不蒐集姓名、電子郵件地址、電話號碼、身分證字號或任何可直接識別個人身分的資料。

二、Cookie 與網站使用資訊

本站使用第三方服務放送廣告與分析流量，這些服務可能會在您的裝置上存放 Cookie，並蒐集不直接識別個人身分的技術資訊，例如瀏覽器類型、作業系統、裝置類型、來源網頁、瀏覽的頁面與停留時間，以及概略的地理位置（通常僅到城市層級）。

三、Google AdSense 與第三方廣告

本站使用 Google AdSense 放送廣告。相關說明如下：

  • Google 作為第三方供應商，會使用 Cookie 在本站放送廣告。
  • Google 使用廣告 Cookie，可根據使用者先前造訪本站與其他網站的紀錄放送廣告。
  • 使用者可前往 Google 的廣告設定頁面（google.com/settings/ads）選擇停用個人化廣告。
  • 亦可前往 aboutads.info 選擇停用部分第三方供應商的個人化廣告 Cookie。

本站無法控制第三方廣告供應商所使用的 Cookie，也無法存取這些 Cookie 的內容。

四、歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的使用者

對於位於歐洲經濟區、英國及瑞士的訪客，本站採用 Google 認證的同意聲明管理平台（CMP），在放送個人化廣告前徵求您的同意。您可以隨時透過該同意聲明介面變更或撤回您的選擇。

五、資料保存

本站本身不保存任何使用者資料。第三方服務的資料保存期限依其各自的隱私權政策辦理，本站無法代為刪除。

六、兒童隱私

本站的內容以大專院校學生與工程從業人員為對象，不刻意蒐集未滿十三歲兒童的任何資料。若您發現有此類情形，請透過聯絡頁面告知，本站將盡快處理。

七、外部連結

本站可能連結至外部網站。這些網站有各自的隱私權政策，本站對其內容與做法不負責任，建議您在提供任何資料前先行閱讀。

八、政策變更

本政策可能因功能調整或法規變動而修改。修改後將更新本頁上方的日期，重大變更會在首頁另行公告。

九、聯絡方式

若您對本政策有任何疑問，請透過聯絡頁面與我們聯繫。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

This page explains how Suanzhi (suanzhii.netlify.app, "the site") handles user data. By using the site you agree to the practices described here.

1. What the site does not collect

All calculators run entirely in your browser. Any values you enter — grades, credits, soil parameters, engineering figures — are never transmitted to this site or to any third party, and are never stored or logged.

The site has no accounts and does not request or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, government identifiers or any other directly identifying personal information.

2. Cookies and usage information

The site uses third-party services to serve advertising and measure traffic. These services may place cookies on your device and collect non-identifying technical information such as browser type, operating system, device type, referring page, pages viewed, time spent and approximate location, generally no more precise than city level.

3. Google AdSense and third-party advertising

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at google.com/settings/ads.
  • You may also opt out of personalised advertising cookies from participating third-party vendors at aboutads.info.

This site does not control and cannot access cookies used by third-party advertising vendors.

4. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

For visitors located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the site uses a Google-certified Consent Management Platform to obtain consent before serving personalised advertising. You may change or withdraw your choice at any time through that consent interface.

5. Data retention

This site retains no user data of its own. Retention by third-party services is governed by their respective privacy policies and cannot be managed on your behalf here.

6. Children's privacy

The site is aimed at university students and engineering practitioners and does not knowingly collect information from children under thirteen. If you believe this has occurred, please get in touch through the contact page.

7. External links

The site may link to external websites, each with its own privacy policy. This site is not responsible for their content or practices.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as features or regulations change. The date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
