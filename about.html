關於本站

算紙（Suanzhi）是一個給理工科學生使用的線上計算工具站。做這個站的起點很簡單：寫作業或查資料時，網路上找得到公式，也找得到答案，但幾乎找不到「把數字代進公式的那一步」。而學生最常卡住的，恰恰是那一步。

所以這裡每個工具都不只給結果，會把係數怎麼算、數字怎麼代、單位怎麼換，一行一行列出來。你可以拿它檢查自己的計算過程，而不是只拿一個無法驗證的數字。

誰在維護

本站由一位就讀地球科學相關系所的學生獨自製作與維護，課程範圍涵蓋大地工程、流體力學、物理與資料科學，並有透地雷達（GPR）的實作經驗。工具的公式來源以課本與現行工程規範為準，程式碼與計算邏輯都是自行撰寫。

目前提供的工具

  • GPA 換算與加權平均：台灣百分制轉四分制、4.3 制、美式等第與澳洲 WAM
  • Rankine 主動與被動土壓力：支援凝聚力、地表超載與地下水位
  • 應力與壓力單位換算：kPa、MPa、kgf/cm²、tf/m²、psi、bar

之後會陸續加入單樁承載力、標準貫入試驗 N 值修正、雷諾數判定、畢業學分試算等工具。

使用上的提醒

所有計算都在你的瀏覽器內完成，輸入的數值不會被傳送到任何伺服器，也不會被儲存。這一方面是為了隱私，另一方面也讓工具在網路不穩時仍然可用。

本站的計算結果適合用於學習、作業檢核與初步估算。實際工程設計必須依照現行規範辦理並經由專業技師簽證；GPA 換算則因各校標準不同，申請前請務必確認校方公告的換算方式。

English

About

Suanzhi is a set of free online calculators for students in engineering and the earth sciences. The idea came from a simple gap: formulas are easy to find online, and so are answers, but the step in between — actually substituting the numbers into the formula — is almost never shown. That step is where students get stuck.

Every tool here lists the working: how each coefficient is derived, which numbers go where, and how the units convert. You can check your own calculation against it rather than trusting a number you cannot verify.

Who maintains it

The site is built and maintained by a single student in an earth sciences programme, with coursework spanning geotechnical engineering, fluid mechanics, physics and data science, and hands-on experience with ground penetrating radar. Formulas follow standard textbooks and current engineering codes; all code and calculation logic is written from scratch.

Current tools

  • Percentage to GPA conversion for Asian transcripts, including 4.0, 4.3, US letter and Australian WAM
  • Rankine active and passive earth pressure with cohesion, surcharge and water table
  • Stress and pressure unit conversion

Notes on use

All calculations run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server or stored anywhere. Results are intended for study, homework checking and preliminary estimation. Engineering work must be reviewed by a licensed engineer, and GPA conversions must be confirmed against the published standard of the institution you are applying to.

計算結果僅供學習與初步估算參考。工程計算請依現行規範辦理，並由專業技師複核；GPA 換算請以申請學校公告的標準為準。
